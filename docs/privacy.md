# Privacy

- Camera permission is requested only after the user taps the start action.
- Native camera buffers are analyzed on-device. Only landmarks cross into JavaScript; up to 200 landmark samples are held briefly for isolated-word recognition, or up to 400 native samples resampled to 96 / 12 seconds for sentence translation. Sentence landmark buffers are cleared after translation, cancellation, interruption or leaving the mode. No photos or video files are created by this flow. Native model weights may stay cached for 30 seconds after stopping; no app-owned camera frames are retained in that cache.
- Personal signs and Recognition Lab are optional. Tapping Record example collects one feature sequence; only Save example persists it in the app's Documents directory (`signbridge-examples-v1.json`, up to 120 records). Records contain normalized hand/body/face features, observation masks, timing, expected label, signer nickname, session/split, gesture-version IDs, available predictions, per-model leaders and capture diagnostics. Empty predictions explicitly indicate unavailable model output. Facial geometry is sensitive even without video; use pseudonyms and export only recordings you intend to share. Ordinary signing does not save examples. Personal learning uses these local features; no server receives them. Save & use this version saves the active signer and enabled setting in the same file, so the profile can be rebuilt locally on reopening the camera screen. Turning personal learning off persists that choice. Deleting examples clears the active profile.
- Remove last and Delete all remove saved examples; Delete all also clears a failed-write temporary file. Export examples writes a JSON file to the folder explicitly chosen in the system picker. Copies in that folder are outside the app's Delete all action; a cloud-backed folder may sync according to the user's file-provider settings. Nothing is automatically exported or uploaded.
- In translator mode, camera frames are not uploaded and video is not recorded. In video calls, the local camera stream and any enabled microphone audio are transmitted to the invited guest using WebRTC. A TURN relay may carry encrypted traffic when a direct connection is unavailable. TalkBridge does not record calls or log image data.
- Microphone access is optional. It is requested when the caller chooses to enable audio; calls start muted by default. Signing and typed captions do not require microphone permission.
- Call invitations are randomly generated bearer codes for two-person rooms. The signaling service sees display names, connection metadata, and network addresses. It keeps rooms in memory, does not persist call records or captions, and expires invitations after 15 minutes of waiting. Display names are not verified identities. Operational proxy logs are controlled by the deployment operator.
- AI caption recognition runs locally on the same video frames used by the call. Landmark features are not sent to the guest or signaling server. Reviewed captions, explicitly enabled automatic AI guesses, and typed captions are sent over the encrypted peer data channel. Automatic guesses are labeled unreviewed. TalkBridge holds the latest captions in memory and clears them at hang-up.
- The transcript exists only in memory during the current app session. It is not stored as history and is cleared when the app process ends.
- Copying text explicitly places the selected transcript on the system clipboard.
- Spoken output is optional. Device voice uses the phone's speech engine without sending text to TalkBridge's voice service; offline availability and synthesis behavior depend on the installed engine. Choosing an online male, female, or personal voice and tapping Speak, or explicitly enabling automatic speech for accepted signs, sends that text to the TalkBridge voice service and ElevenLabs. The app does not silently send unfinished recognition guesses or typed keystrokes for speech. The service does not persist speech request text. ElevenLabs request retention follows the connected account's policy.
- Creating a personal voice requires selecting a speaking video, confirming speaker authorization, and tapping Create my voice. The app uploads the video to the service, which extracts up to 120 seconds of audio locally and sends only that audio to ElevenLabs for cloning. Temporary media on the service and the app's picker copy are removed after processing or failure; the original user recording remains. The provider retains the cloned voice and training sample until deletion. A personal voice is not created from ordinary live signing or a video call.
- Voice choice, speed, and automatic speech preference are saved in the app's Documents directory. The voice service access key and a random device ownership capability are saved in the OS secure store. The backend persists a private profile containing provider voice ID, name, authorization version, creation time, and verification state, indexed by a hash of the capability. Personal voice access requires that device credential; it is not shared with a call guest. Deleting a voice removes it at the provider before removing its backend profile. A failed deletion remains retryable.
- Meeting speech is optional. Tapping Speak to meeting, or enabling Speak reviewed signs and sharing a reviewed result, sends the chosen text to the speaker's voice service and ElevenLabs. Generated audio reaches the guest through the encrypted WebRTC data channel. The guest receives no personal voice ID, provider key, device credential, or audio download URL. The signaling service does not receive this speech audio. Unreviewed guesses and private previews are not spoken. Hearing incoming speech can be turned off independently of the microphone.
- Generated speech audio is held briefly in the app cache for playback and removed afterward. Stop, Clear, navigation away, changing voices, and backgrounding cancel queued translator speech. Meeting Stop voice and hang-up stop queued meeting speech; Stop also asks the guest to stop the sender's audio. Meeting audio plays directly on the guest's active audio output, independently of the microphone track.
- Translator camera use pauses when the app backgrounds or the user leaves the camera screen. Video calls end when the app backgrounds or the caller leaves the call screen; their media tracks are released.

The calling feature has a signaling backend; signaling does not perform sign inference. The same service optionally hosts online speech and authorized voice cloning through ElevenLabs. The sentence encoder and English decoder remain bundled local models. Translation does not upload landmarks or unfinished drafts. Release builds do not fall back to a model-download server. Sign model inference runs locally with TensorFlow Lite. See [calling setup](calling.md) and [voice setup](voice.md) for service configuration and deployment boundaries.
